Cookie Policy

Last Updated: April 28, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Connect Mentorship uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your actions and preferences.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Keep you logged in (session management)
  • Improve website performance
  • Understand user behavior and usage patterns
  • Enhance overall user experience

3. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with the site
  • Performance Cookies: Improve speed and performance

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services such as:

  • Google Analytics
  • Meta (Facebook) Pixel

These services may collect information about your interaction with our website.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect some features of the website.

6. Consent

By continuing to use our website, you agree to our use of cookies.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
