Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 28, 2026

Connect Mentorship respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect your data when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, if provided
  • Questions, answers, replies, mentor applications, and contact messages
  • Login and account information through Firebase Authentication
  • Basic analytics data such as browser, device, and page activity

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Display questions, answers, and mentor profiles
  • Review mentor applications
  • Improve platform features and user experience
  • Prevent spam, abuse, and misuse
  • Contact users for support or important updates

3. Public Content

Questions, answers, replies, mentor names, and verified mentor profiles may be publicly visible on the platform. Please do not share sensitive personal information in public posts.

4. Data Storage

We use Firebase and Google Cloud services to store account and platform data. We take reasonable steps to protect your data from unauthorized access.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only when required by law, to protect platform safety, or to operate necessary services such as Firebase.

6. Cookies and Analytics

We may use cookies, Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar tools to understand user behavior, improve the website, and measure performance.

7. User Rights

You may request to:

  • Update your profile information
  • Delete your account
  • Remove your submitted content, where possible
  • Contact us about privacy concerns

8. Children and Students

Connect Mentorship is designed for students. If you are under the age required by local law, please use the platform with permission from a parent or guardian.

9. Security

We use Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules to help protect user data. However, no online platform can guarantee complete security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
