Mentor Policy

Mentors on Connect Mentorship are expected to guide students honestly, respectfully, and professionally.

1. Mentor Verification

Mentor applications may be reviewed before profiles are approved. Verification only confirms platform approval, not guaranteed expertise or outcomes.

2. Honest Guidance

Mentors must not make false promises about marks, jobs, income, admissions, or guaranteed success.

3. No Off-Platform Contact

Mentors must not ask students to share phone numbers, emails, WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, UPI, or other direct contact details.

4. Professional Behaviour

Mentors must communicate respectfully and avoid harassment, abuse, discrimination, misleading advice, or inappropriate content.

5. Paid Sessions

Paid chat or call sessions must happen only through Connect Mentorship’s platform tools after payment verification and admin/session approval.

6. Policy Violations

Connect Mentorship may warn, suspend, unverify, or ban mentors who violate platform rules.
