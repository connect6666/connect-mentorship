Why Become a Mentor?

Mentorship helps students avoid common mistakes and make informed decisions. On Connect, mentors share real-life experiences related to exams, college life, and career paths.

Becoming a mentor allows you to help students who are confused about their future. You can share your real experiences and guide juniors in making better academic and career decisions.

It also helps you build your profile, gain experience, and contribute to the student community.
