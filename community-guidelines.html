Community Guidelines

Connect Mentorship is a safe platform for students and mentors. All users must follow these guidelines.

1. Respect Everyone

Be polite and respectful. Do not use abusive, offensive, or harmful language.

2. No Spam

Do not post repeated messages, promotions, or irrelevant content.

3. No Fake Information

Do not share false, misleading, or harmful advice.

4. No Personal Data Sharing

Do not share phone numbers, emails, or social media links in chats or questions.

5. Safe Content Only

Avoid adult content, illegal topics, hate speech, or harmful discussions.

6. Platform Usage

Use the platform for learning, guidance, and career support only.

7. Violations

Violating these rules may lead to warnings, account suspension, or permanent ban.
